# Submission requirements and useful offers

Verified from the official [Project Submission Guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/) in the browser on 14 November 2026; Singapore schedule also supplied by the participant.

- Deadline: Sunday 15 November 2026, 23:59 SGT. Working target: 22:00 SGT; aim for readiness by 18:00.
- Demo: video no longer than 30 seconds OR no more than seven slides, including title and introduction.
- Demo and project links must be public without login, permission requests or registration. Test anonymous access.
- English project text and slides; non-English video speech requires English subtitles.
- Include NASA data/resources actually used, partner/complementary resources, tools and references.
- Disclose where and how AI/SI tools assisted; acknowledge generated code in descriptive text/metadata. Do not misattribute NASA observations as AI-generated data.
- Include team name, member names and selected challenge. Team registration/acceptance must be confirmed by participants.
- Saving a draft is not submission. The official form's final submit action has not been taken.
